Our Story

Built out of a real frustration.
For a very real problem.

We didn't start with a whiteboard and a business plan. We started with a missed appointment, an awkward text, and a stylist who deserved better tools.

The moment that started it all

Like a lot of people, life got busy. Work deadlines, kids' schedules, the hundred small things that stack up during a week. And somewhere in that chaos, an appointment would slip — and the guilt would set in.

Not just the guilt of missing the appointment itself, but the awkwardness of having to reach out after hours to a stylist's personal phone to cancel or reschedule. Knowing they were probably winding down for the evening. Knowing they'd have to update a notebook, move things around, and wonder if that slot would fill again.

"The stylist wasn't just losing a booking — she was losing time, peace of mind, and control over her own schedule. And there was no easy way to fix it."

That moment made something clear: independent stylists were running their businesses on personal phones, paper notebooks, and manual follow-ups — not because they wanted to, but because the tools available were either too expensive, too complicated, or just not built for them.

So a small team set out to fix it. Not with another clunky scheduling software. With something that felt like having a smart, always-on assistant — one that could handle bookings, reminders, and cancellations so stylists never had to interrupt their evening again.

That's Salon Assist CX.

A booking platform powered by AI that works the way stylists actually work — from a shareable link, with no per-booking fees, no complicated setup, and no more chasing clients at 10pm.

It's scheduling that disappears into the background so the relationship between stylist and client can move to the foreground — where it belongs.

Our mission

We exist to help independent stylists build stronger client relationships by removing the logistical bottlenecks that get in the way — so that a busy schedule, a last-minute cancel, or a late-night reschedule never has to damage something that took years to build.

The best stylists aren't just good with hair. They're trusted, remembered, and referred. We want every one of them to have the tools to stay that way — without burning out managing the back end of their business alone.

A small team. A clear purpose.

We're a small, focused team that believes the best products come from people who understand the problem personally. We built Salon Assist CX because we lived the frustration — and we stay close to our stylists because their feedback shapes everything we build next.
